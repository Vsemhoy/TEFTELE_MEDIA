# Я выбираю себя

Glossy 80s-style synth-pop, female vocals purring over bright arpeggiated keys and soft gated drums. Verses stay intimate with warm pads and subtle chorus guitar; choruses bloom into airy harmonies, sparkly bell hooks, and a gently driving bass line. Energy arcs from tender reflection to shimmering, danceable optimism by the final chorus.
